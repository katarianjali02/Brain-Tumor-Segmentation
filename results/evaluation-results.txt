Brain Tumor Segmentation - Evaluation Results

Datasets:
- BraTS 2021
- BraTS 2020

BraTS 2021 Dataset Performance:
Whole Tumor (WT): 92.0%
Tumor Core (TC): 87.0%
Enhancing Tumor (ET): 79.0%

BraTS 2020 Dataset Performance:
Whole Tumor (WT): 90.0%
Tumor Core (TC): 85.0%
Enhancing Tumor (ET): 73.0%

Post-processing:
3D morphological closing and a 400-voxel thresholding rule were applied
to the Enhancing Tumor regions to reduce isolated false-positive noise.

Note:
BraTS 2021 was used for the primary development/evaluation pipeline,
while BraTS 2020 was used for additional testing of the trained model.
